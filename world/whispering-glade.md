# The Whispering Glade

**Type**: World Part / Hidden Realm (New Connection)

**Status**: Discovered Ley Line Nexus

## Description
The Whispering Glade is a hidden, emotion-responsive forest realm nestled within the veil-adjacent layers of the Shattered Continent. Its flora and fauna physically manifest the emotional states of visitors — blooming with beautiful luminescent flowers during joy or peace, or twisting into thorny, aggressive forms during grief or rage. Ancient stone ruins and glowing rune circles suggest it was once a sanctuary for the Weave Keepers. The glade serves as a place of reflection, testing, and potential peril.

## Connections
- **Ley Line to Shattered Spire**: A newly discovered stable connection allowing attuned Luminari to travel instantaneously between the two locations.
- **Veil Proximity**: High risk of attracting Veilborn creatures like the Thornveil Serpent when strong negative emotions are present.
- **Ethereal Weave Thread**: Strengthens the overall network and reveals hints of lost temples and ancient knowledge.

# Arcanea Universe Bible

## Overview
Arcanea is a living, sovereign creative universe of superheroes (Luminari), Veilborn creatures, interconnected realms, and epic stories. It emphasizes emotional depth, visual consistency, and daily evolution. All elements tie into the Ethereal Weave — the cosmic fabric connecting worlds, powers, and fates.

## Visual Canon (Strictly Enforced for All Generations)
- **Color Palette**: Deep premium blacks as dominant base (#0A0A0F, #111117). Accents in vibrant cyan (#00F0FF), teal (#00B4A0), and luminous gold (#FFD700, #E6B800). Subtle silver and ethereal white highlights.
- **Lighting**: Volumetric dramatic lighting with strong god rays, rim lighting, deep shadows, and glowing energy effects. Cinematic high-contrast moody atmospheres. Glowing cyan/teal energy veins or gold runes on characters/creatures.
- **Style**: Hyper-detailed, cinematic, photorealistic-fantasy fusion. Rich textures (fur, armor, fabric, stone). No text, logos, or watermarks unless explicitly specified. Maintain **exact identity** across all images (face, body proportions, clothing, color scheme, pose language).
- **Composition**: Epic scales, dynamic action poses, layered depth, ethereal particles, floating elements. Premium dark aesthetic with high production value.
- **Consistency Rules**: Always reference prior images for face, body, clothing, style, and composition. Use the same Visual DNA block verbatim in prompts.

## Core Lore Pillars
- **Luminari (Superheroes)**: Gifted protectors bonded to the Weave. Powers often elemental, conceptual, or emotional. They evolve through trials and connections.
- **Veilborn (Creatures)**: Beings born from tears in the veil between realities. They range from feral predators to wise guardians. Many have symbiotic or antagonistic relationships with Luminari.
- **World & Realms**: The primary realm is the Shattered Continent, with floating citadels, veiled forests, and nexus points. Connections via portals, ley lines, and emotional bonds.
- **Stories**: Ongoing narrative threads of incursion, alliance, betrayal, discovery, and growth. Daily beats advance the larger saga.

## Key Factions & Threads
- The Luminari Council
- Veilborn Hordes and independent packs
- The Weave Keepers (ancient order)
- Emerging threats from the Outer Void

# Astral Vanguard

**Type**: Luminari Superhero (New)

**Status**: Active Protector, Newly Bonded to the Weave

## Description
Astral Vanguard is a Luminari who channels the raw power of distant stars and constellations through a fallen star fragment embedded in his armor. He represents hope and guidance in the darkness, using stellar energy to forge weapons and barriers. His arrival coincides with new threats from the Outer Void, making him a key ally in defending the Ethereal Weave connections.

## Abilities
- **Starforge**: Manifests weapons, shields, and projectiles from condensed gold starlight energy.
- **Constellation Shift**: Dissolves into a swarm of glowing points to teleport short distances or evade attacks.
- **Nova Pulse**: Releases a radial burst of cyan and teal stellar energy for area control and crowd dispersal.
- **Weave Attunement**: Heightened sensitivity to ley lines and portal instability.

## Connections to Existing Lore
- Teams with Lumina Weaver for the Glade incursion.
- Bonded to the same Weave network as other Luminari.
- Potential council member or field leader.

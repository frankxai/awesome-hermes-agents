# Thornveil Serpent

**Type**: Veilborn Creature (New)

**Status**: Active Incursion Threat / Potential Ally

## Description
The Thornveil Serpent is a serpentine Veilborn that embodies the duality of pain and beauty in emotional turmoil. Born from tears in the veil triggered by intense negative emotions, it feeds on emotional energy but can be purified and bonded when a Luminari demonstrates emotional mastery. It has a sinuous, elegant form and serves as both predator and mirror to the inner struggles of heroes.

## Abilities
- **Thorn Projection**: Launches aether-infused thorns that create emotional feedback loops, amplifying fear, grief, or anger in targets.
- **Veil Slip**: Phases through the veil for short bursts of invisibility and repositioning.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Passively amplifies or dampens emotions in a radius, causing flora or allies to react.
- **Purification Bond** (latent): When bonded with a Luminari, can convert negative energy into healing or protective fields.

## Connections to Existing Lore
- Attracted to the Whispering Glade's emotional manifestations.
- Potential evolution or rival to Aetherclaw Veilborn packs.
- Story hook for Lumina Weaver and Astral Vanguard alliance.
